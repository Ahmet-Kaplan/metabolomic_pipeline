7. L4-no-floor-survivor failure diagnostic

07_floor_failure_breakdown

For features whose candidates ALL failed the quality floor, which gate did the work? Score-fail-only candidates would be rescued by lowering the Score floor; |ppm|-fail-only by loosening the mass-error floor. Cross-fails have near-passes on each axis but on different candidates. Both-fail features are genuinely junk-quality with no realistic rescue path. If one bar dominates, the corresponding floor may be too tight.
